At Nellis Architecture, we believe that great design is driven by meaningful collaboration. In a recent video feature, Lee Nellis, Founder of Nellis Architecture, and James Sharples, Projects Director at Bauhaus, [discuss the power of shared expertise in shaping successful projects.](https://youtu.be/0F9dgGYiV2A?si=SddAOBM2G49Gyxak)

The conversation explores a range of topics, including Soul—our standout development on La Mer—highlighting the unique features that set it apart.

They also reflect on the broader impact of thoughtful design and project management in creating exceptional spaces for both homeowners and investors, underscoring the importance of alignment between creative vision and execution.
