Today, we celebrate our first year anniversary, and what a journey it’s been.

One year.

365 days of bold ideas and fast growth.

25 projects across the world: UAE, Greece, Russia, Scotland, and India.

Countless hours of hard work, endless creativity, and so many shared laughs.

 

We’re truly grateful for each member of our team.

Here’s to the next chapter of authentic passion, artisanal craft, and abiding purpose.
