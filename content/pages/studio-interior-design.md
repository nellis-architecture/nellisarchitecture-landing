# Interior design

Our luxury interior design shapes how a family lives in a house. Villa interior design in Dubai does not begin when the shell is complete. It begins with how a family moves through a home, how they gather and how they want to feel.

## Interior and architecture as one

Interior design is not a layer applied to architecture. We develop the structure and the interior at the same time, so spaces flow, ceilings rise where they are needed and columns stay out of the way. Because we also design the architecture, the house and its rooms speak the same language.

## Light, views and materials

On Palm Jumeirah, orientation defines everything. We map how light moves through each room during the day, soften it with overhangs, recesses and screens, and layer artificial light for the evening. We choose stone, timber and metal for how they perform in a coastal climate, so the interior stays calm and resolved for years.
