# Master planning

Our master plan design turns land into communities: residential neighbourhoods, gated villa communities, a hotel and new uses for unused land.

## How we plan

Architecture, landscape and planning develop together. A master plan considers how people will live, move and gather in a place, and how buildings, streets and open spaces work with the climate.

## Selected projects

- [WEST LINKS](/projects/master-planning/west-links/): a residential community next to a Scottish golf club
- [Evergreens](/projects/master-planning/evergreens/): a master-planned gated community of luxury villas in Hyderabad, India
- [Evergreens Hotel](/projects/master-planning/evergreens-hotel/)
