People

We are a cluster of courageous master craftsmen (and women), brave artists, extremely hard-working doers, creative dreamers and architectural makers.

Each team member contributes their unique passion, innovation and creative aesthetic to our rapidly growing studio

Anna Saunkina

Creative Curator
With roots in Moscow's dynamic energy and wings that have carried her across the world, Anna brings a uniquely artistic vision to her role as Social & Creative Marketing Consultant. Her journey is a beautiful blend of diverse passions - from the precise elegance of mathematics to the fluid grace of ballet, from captivating photography to compelling TV ads. When she's not crafting inspiring marketing strategies, you might find her exploring new ways to enhance human well-being or discovering fresh creative perspectives. Anna's adaptable nature and global mindset (she'll tell you the whole world feels like home) allows her to connect authentically with diverse audiences, while her commitment to raising the bar in everything she does drives our creative projects to new heights. Her belief that great design can influence how people think, feel, and evolve makes her the perfect person to tell our architectural stories in ways that truly resonate and inspire.

Ashley Bothma

Associate Architect
If you're looking for someone who embodies the spirit of adventure in architecture, that's Ashley! Our globe-trotting architect's journey from the small towns of South Africa to the bustling streets of Japan (and now Dubai!) has shaped his wonderfully unique approach to design. His love for architecture was born in a small beach town in the Eastern Cape, where his grandfather's handcrafted family home became the stage for his childhood memories - and sparked a lifelong passion for creating spaces where beautiful moments unfold. When he's not designing memory-making spaces, you'll find Ashley scaling rock climbing walls, perfecting his golf swing, or seeking out new adventures. His ever-present smile and ability to find silver linings in any situation bring a special energy to our team. As a self-proclaimed 'jack of all trades,' Ashley brings an eclectic perspective to every project, drawing inspiration from his nomadic life experiences and his belief that architecture is the ultimate stage setting for life's most precious moments.

Austine Lizarondo

Architect
Two influences have shaped who Austine is today: mentors who encouraged his growth in both skill and mindset, helping him become a more thoughtful and confident leader, and his passion for dance, which taught him discipline, creative expression, and how to see rhythm and storytelling in space. Dancing is where Austine feels most himself and where he learned to express emotion, connect with people, and be fully present. He believes the most meaningful designs come from understanding people, a value he was reminded of while working on a recent community project. Born and raised in the Philippines, a place of warmth and community, Austine finds inspiration in everyday moments: street sounds, shared meals, and the ability of people to smile through anything. That spirit keeps him grounded and creative, not only in his work but also in dance, graphic design and photography. To Austine, architecture is storytelling through space, and he loves being part of Nellis Architecture, where he can connect with the community and turn someone’s dream into a reality.

Fiona Webb-Johnston

Interior Architecture Lead
Raised in New Zealand’s Bay of Islands, Fiona grew up surrounded by stunning natural landscapes and coastline. Living overseas made her appreciate this environment even more, as well as the importance of family, friendships, and home. Throughout her career, Fiona’s colleagues and experiences have shaped her professionally and personally. Early on, supportive leaders encouraged her to think bigger, pursue further training, and move beyond her comfort zone. Their support led to opportunities such as serving on the National Lighting Society Committee and helping to organise the National Design Awards. Creativity has always been central to Fiona’s life, drawing her to design in many forms, from interiors, architecture, and lighting to objects, art, and fashion. Working in New Zealand, the United States, and the UAE has broadened her perspective and deepened her appreciation for collaboration in diverse teams. At Nellis Architecture, Fiona is excited to be part of a young studio with incredible projects and integral to its strong growth ahead, helping shape the next stage of its journey.

Franklin De Guzman

Visual Architect
Say hello to Franklin, our 3D visualization wizard who turns architectural dreams into jaw-dropping reality. With two decades of experience under his belt, he's the genius who makes our designs look so real you'd swear you could walk right into them. When he's not working his magic on stunning visualizations, you'll find Franklin geeking out over his impressive art toy collection. Having Franklin on our team is like having a Hollywood special effects master, but for architecture... and with way more art toys!"

Ilia Mishchenko

Senior Interior Designer
Ilia is the kind of designer who travels with a measuring tape. In restaurants, hotels, and public spaces, he sketches proportions and notes details. The habit reflects a larger philosophy: to look beyond the surface and understand the reasoning behind every design. Ilia was born in Krasnodar, in southern Russia. His path to interior design began with LEGO, then art school, then a formal education in engineering and architecture. Early career years in residential construction and furniture design rounded out his understanding. He learned how a great idea becomes a real, finished space. Now based in Dubai, he leads refined residential and villa projects at Nellis Architecture. The ambition of each brief matches his appetite for thoughtful, fully resolved work. His approach is unmistakably his own. It is equal parts creative thinking and engineering rigour. He is drawn to interiors that are beautiful and buildable, refined and practical, considered down to the last junction. For Ilia, design and architecture are inseparable. The most rewarding part of any project is the moment a drawing becomes a place where someone will live.

Ivy Corpuz

Technical Architect
There's something special about an experienced architect who understands both innovation and roots - that's Ivy! Her approach to design has been shaped by rich conversations with fellow architects and hands-on experience, always driven by a curiosity that runs deep. When she's not creating spaces that stir emotions, you'll find her on the tennis court. Ivy brings a unique blend of boldness and thoughtfulness to our team, always remembering that great architecture isn't just about what catches the eye - it's about how it makes you feel when you move through a carefully crafted space

Kateryna Shekhovtsova

Studio Narrative Curator
Meet Katya, our operations guru who keeps Nellis Architecture running like a well-oiled machine! With years of globe-trotting experience in hospitality, she brings that special touch of warmth to everything we do. If you're wondering where her knack for creating feel-good vibes comes from, it all started with lively family gatherings and shared meals that taught her the magic of bringing people together. When she's not masterfully coordinating our team and projects, you'll find her exploring art galleries, discovering new places, or diving into the stories behind everything she encounters. Katya's love for different cultures and creative expression makes her the perfect fit for supporting our diverse architectural projects, keeping our operations smooth and our team connected.

Lee Nellis

Principal Architect and Founder
As the founder of Nellis Architecture, Lee brings more than just his Scottish roots and RIBA/RIAS credentials to Dubai's architectural landscape - he brings heart! Since 2010, he's been crafting not just buildings, but lasting relationships that extend far beyond the final blueprint. Lee's philosophy perfectly balances traditional craftsmanship with technological innovation, creating spaces that tell meaningful stories and touch lives. When he's not transforming architectural dreams into reality, you'll find him deeply engaged in his passion for football - watching, playing, or diving into the latest match analysis. What truly sets Lee apart is how he treasures the human side of architecture; there's nothing he loves more than being invited back years later to share a meal in a home he helped create, seeing how his designs have become part of families' stories.

Margarita Ataekgaeva

3D Visualizer
Observation has always shaped Margarita’s approach to personal growth and projects. She enjoys studying workflows across different environments, teams, and projects, noticing details, and analysing both her own and others’ mistakes. These observations, turned into solutions and then applied in practice, are among the most valuable ways Margarita feels she fuels her professional growth. Paired with her passions outside of work, Margarita constantly sharpens her visual sensitivity and influence through practising phone photography and exploring the worlds of cinema, literature and fashion, to challenge and change the way she thinks about atmosphere, composition, and storytelling in her work as a 3D artist. From Belarus, a place where the weather and seasons are constantly changing, Margarita has always been fascinated by the natural, dynamic sense of movement and contrast, which has shaped how she sees spaces and environments. To her, architecture is a balance between emotion and logic, with the most successful spaces telling a story and creating an atmosphere that people can feel. This is a sentiment Margarita feels is also appreciated by Nellis Architecture, where design quality, aesthetics, and attention to detail truly matter and are clearly valued.

Matas Belevicius

Associate Architect
Long before he knew what architecture was, as a child, Matas spent hours building LEGO structures, proudly displaying each creation for weeks. This same childhood curiosity in creation eventually led him toward design. Born surrounded by nature in Kaunas, Lithuania, Matas found simplicity, calm, and connection while outdoors, camping by a quiet lake or kayaking along winding rivers. He credits his hometown with instilling a strong sense of community and appreciation for spaces that feel accessible, intimate, and human. At Newcastle University, Matas turned curiosity into critical thinking, learning from tutors who encouraged him to explore ideas fearlessly, question everything, and focus not just on how a building looks, but why. Early in his career, he learned that design excellence requires discipline behind creativity, the value of details, and the beauty of teamwork. Wanderlust led Matas to explore diverse environments and cultures, shaping his belief that architecture must respond to its context, with an appreciation for simplicity and functionality, and a harmony shaped by people, purpose, and perspective. At Nellis Architecture, he admires the team’s focus on artisanal craft, authenticity, and purposeful design and values the freedom given to bring curiosity to every project.

Shamel's calm and focused nature, combined with an unwavering ambition, brings a grounding presence to our team. When not crafting purposeful spaces, you'll find him cycling through nature trails, capturing moments through his camera lens, or exploring new destinations - experiences that continuously inspire his architectural vision.

When he's not crafting environments that enhance quality of life, you'll find him behind his camera lens, capturing subtle details that others might miss - a skill that beautifully influences his architectural perspective. Living in Dubai, Paul draws endless inspiration from the city's dynamic blend of cultures and innovation, channeling this energy into his detail-oriented approach to design.

When he's not crafting innovative spaces or collaborating with fellow creatives, you'll find him cycling outdoor trails, perfecting his golf swing, or cooking up something delicious - that is, if he's not busy having adventures with his two beautiful daughters! Ramel Borres

Craft Architect
Born and raised in Manila, Philippines, Ramel was shaped by his mother’s determination to work tirelessly to send her children to school. He credits her resilience as his motivation to build a better future for himself and his family. As a young boy, Ramel loved to draw. What started as a simple hobby evolved into a means of training his eye for detail and refining his perspective on the world. This passion naturally evolved into a love for architecture and creating spaces that tell stories and preserve history. Ramel has always been fascinated by Manila’s old Spanish-era houses, their intricate designs reminding him that beauty often lies in the details and the stories behind them. Though one of the quieter members of the team, Ramel is always listening, learning, and fuelling his creativity, finding that every day at Nellis Architecture brings an opportunity to be inspired by the talented team around him.

Sasha Kiseleva

Operations and Culture
From the cultural richness of Moscow to the dynamic energy of the Middle East, Sasha brings a wealth of cross-sector experience to her role as Executive Director at Nellis Architecture. Since moving to the region in 2013, she's built an impressive career spanning different industries, bringing fresh perspectives and innovative approaches to everything she touches. When she's not championing our team's growth or exploring the depths of human psychology, you'll find her embracing wellness through vegan cuisine and holistic living. Sasha's hunger for constant learning and her courage to question the status quo have shaped Nellis Architecture into more than just a business - it's become a place where technical excellence meets artistic soul, where profits take a backseat to purpose. Her favorite quote from Émile Zola, 'I came to live out loud,' perfectly captures how she approaches both life and leadership, creating an environment where architecture becomes a celebration of human potential and meaningful connections.

Siebert Labuschagne

Interior Design & Architectural Technician
Siebert sees South Africa, the country where he was born and raised and still calls home today, as defined by diversity. Growing up in an environment with varied languages, landscapes, food, and cultures has shaped how Siebert sees the world, with perceived differences influencing his approach to architecture, always considering how spaces serve different people and purposes. Professionally, his biggest influence has been working across different scales and stages of projects. Experiencing projects from early design through to detailed technical documentation has shown him how essential structure, coordination, and communication are in delivering good architecture. His personal passions for photography and traditional carpentry, whilst practised outside the office, have also lent themselves to his work, sharpening his eye for clean composition and structure, and helping him understand how models will eventually be constructed when building things by hand. At Nellis Architecture, Siebert’s role focuses on Revit modelling and technical documentation. In his role, clarity and accuracy are critical to advancing a project from concept to construction. He relishes the studio’s collaborative environment where his colleagues value both his design and technical precision, and encourage him to continue improving and producing work that is clear, coordinated, and purposeful.
